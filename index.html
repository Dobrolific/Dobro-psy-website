About Me

Warm, thoughtful care rooted in experience.

Welcome. I'm a licensed psychotherapist dedicated to providing a supportive, non-judgmental space where you can explore your thoughts, feelings, and challenges.

With years of clinical experience, I bring a depth of understanding to every session. My approach integrates evidence-based methods with genuine human connection, because healing happens in relationship.

Whether you're struggling with anxiety, navigating a life transition, or seeking deeper self-understanding, I'm here to walk alongside you on that journey.
